# Turn your data into production apps with GitHub Copilot and Rayfin

**Audience:** AI Tour attendees, level 200 — developers and technical decision makers. Comfortable with building apps and with AI coding assistants; assume little to no Fabric or Rayfin knowledge.
**Duration:** 15 min — 13 min flow + 2 min Act Three (13 slides; Act Two is ~7 min of demo behind 3 bumper slides)
**Goal:** Attendees can explain why prototypes stall at production, and see that a code-first backend on Fabric means the app they vibe-code ships governed and analytics-ready with no rewrite.
**Tone:** Story-driven, following Nadia, a Caldova regional manager, from spreadsheet to governed app. Concrete over promotional.
**Speaker:** Yohan Lasorsa, Principal Cloud Advocate, Microsoft
**Demo:** Mixed — live, with a recording as fallback. Bumper slides must stand alone if the demo is skipped.
**Language:** No retail or pharma jargon. Say "products", "stock running low", "reorder" — never OTC, SKU, sell-through.
**Status:** The pptx is the source of truth for the talk. Scripts for pptx slides 2–5 are locked.

## Act One — Fix the production gap (~6 min)

- Cold open on the before: Nadia's reorder spreadsheet and the email chain around it
- Swap straight to the finished app, live, 20–30s — same job, one governed place
- Show the command and the prompt that started it — built from data already in Fabric
- The demo works, now the questions start: identity, access, governance, audit, cost
- Enterprise apps need more than vibes: the developer list vs the IT admin list
- Get the best of both worlds: Rayfin, code-first backend landing in Fabric on the first write

## Act Two — Turn your data into a real app (~7 min, demo)

- Start from the prompt and the data-app template, real app in seconds
- Company data to app: products running low read straight from Fabric
- Reorder request through a function, written back governed and audited
- Regional dashboard updates from the same estate — closing the loop on the cold open

## Act Three — Key takeaways (2 min)

- Recap: governed from day one, no rewrite step
- Next steps: sessions, learning paths, resources
- Evaluation ask
